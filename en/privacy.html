HEARLINE

Privacy policy

2026-11-16

Hearline processes microphone audio and translation on your device using Apple system services. When Keep recording is enabled before a session, microphone audio is saved locally with that record as M4A audio. The switch is on by default and can be turned off before listening. Text, record names, timestamps, language preferences and the trial balance are also stored locally. Audio files are excluded from device backups by this app; Hearline does not sync them to a server.

After you start listening, the microphone continues when you switch apps while access remains valid. Pause or end listening to stop it. Audio interruptions, an exhausted trial or expired access also stop capture and preserve received text.

Preparing language models requires a connection to Apple. StoreKit contacts Apple to load prices, complete purchases and obtain signed subscription information. Hearline does not operate a server that receives your microphone audio or transcripts, and this version includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Exports leave the app only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. That destination handles the shared file under its own policy. You can play or export a saved recording, or delete its audio while keeping the text. Deleting an entire record also deletes its retained audio; copies you exported elsewhere are separate. Interrupted recording may lose its last, unfinalized audio portion.

The local trial balance is stored separately in the device Keychain. Deleting history does not reset it. Purchase and refund records are managed by Apple. You can manage subscriptions in App Store settings.

For support, email devforall@qq.com. Information you choose to include in your email is used to respond to your request. Hearline does not automatically attach transcripts or audio.

Hearline does not require an app account or use advertising identifiers. You can delete text and audio from Records. Uninstalling removes the app container, but the allowance in the device Keychain, Apple's transaction history and copies you exported are managed separately. Text and preferences may be included in system device backups you enable; the app excludes its recording directory.

If you choose to email support, the email service processes your address and message to let us respond and follow up. You can use the same address to request correction or deletion of support correspondence. Recordings, transcripts and device diagnostics are not attached automatically.

This policy is a static web page without embedded advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and basic access logs to deliver and secure the website; these are not sent by the Hearline app.

Changes to this policy will be dated on this page. Contact devforall@qq.com with questions about Hearline's data handling.
