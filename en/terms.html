Hearline Pro is an auto-renewable subscription. Weekly, monthly and annual plans provide the same Pro features during their verified period of access. The purchase screen shows the actual price, currency and billing period from your App Store. Weekly plans renew every 7 days; monthly and annual plans renew at their respective intervals unless canceled.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account after you confirm the purchase. Manage or cancel renewal in App Store settings. Cancellation normally leaves access available until the current paid period ends; refunds and revocations follow Apple's confirmed transaction status. Deleting Hearline does not cancel a subscription.

The free listening allowance totals 10 minutes and does not renew. It counts time while the microphone is successfully running, including silence, across sessions and languages. Preparation and paused time do not count. At zero, new capture stops and received content is finalized and saved. An unexpected exit may consume up to one second of a reserved interval.

Pro has no commercial minute limit, but device compatibility, installed language models, battery, audio interruptions and operating system behavior still apply. Transcriptions and translations can contain errors and should be checked when accuracy matters.

After access ends, saved text remains readable and manually editable. TXT and Markdown exports remain available. Saved recordings can still be played, exported as M4A and deleted. Keeping audio uses the same listening allowance and does not create a separate unlimited recording mode. Starting further paid work and creating professional subtitle or document exports require active access.

An existing verified subscription works offline until its known expiration or verified billing grace period ends. A connection may be required to confirm a renewal or restore a purchase. The Apple Standard License Agreement also applies.

To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal charges may occur within the final 24 hours of that period. Before using the microphone or keeping recordings, ensure you have permission to record the content and follow applicable consent and privacy requirements.
