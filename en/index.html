Useful export formats

Help & support

Requires iPhone with iOS 26.4 or later and support for the selected language models. Initial model downloads require internet. The interface follows your device language, with 16 language options and English as the fallback. Speech and translation languages are selected separately.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account that originally subscribed, connect to the internet, then tap Restore purchases. If you canceled renewal, Pro normally stays active until the paid period ends.

No prices or purchase unavailable

Check your connection and App Store account, then retry loading prices. Availability depends on your storefront. Unavailable products do not grant Pro or charge you through this app.

Trial or subscription ended

Already received words are preserved. Open the record to review, edit or export TXT/Markdown. Reconnect to confirm renewal, or choose a subscription to start listening again.

Language or microphone problems

Prepare the required language models and allow microphone access. A subscription does not make an unsupported language or device compatible. After an audio interruption, return to Hearline and resume manually.

Billing and refunds

Manage renewal in App Store settings. Apple handles App Store charges and refund requests; use the Apple support link below.

How do I rename or export a record?

In Records, swipe left on a record to rename, edit or export it. Its name becomes the export filename. Swiping right shows no actions.

How do I keep or delete audio?

Keep recording is on by default and can be turned off before a session. Open a finished record to replay, export M4A or delete only its audio. Deleting a record also deletes its audio. Audio is excluded from device backups; export important recordings yourself.

Can listening continue in another app?

After you start a session, microphone transcription and translation can continue when you switch apps. Pause or end to stop. Calls, microphone interruptions or the end of your allowance or access stop capture. Hearline does not directly access calls or another app's internal audio track.

How do the allowance and subscriptions work?

The free listening allowance is a total of 10 minutes and does not refill daily. Weekly, monthly and annual plans auto-renew and provide the same Pro features. Your App Store price is shown before purchase. Reading, manual editing, TXT/Markdown export and playback or M4A export of saved audio do not require renewal.

Contact support

Describe the problem and include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. There is no need to send private recordings or transcripts.
